A private document scanner: offline, no account, no ads

Why AONE Scanner keeps your documents on your phone

The papers people scan most are the ones they'd least like to lose control of: ID cards, certificates, bank letters, medical reports, signed forms. AONE Scanner is built so that none of it needs to leave your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • Scanning and edge detection run on the device, without an internet connection.
  • Your documents and signatures are kept in the app's private storage, not in the gallery and not in a cloud.
  • Editing, signing and making PDFs all happen on the device.
  • No account and no sign-in. There's nothing to register and no ads.

Files leave your phone only when you share them or save them to your Downloads. The app sends us nothing. The page-detection model runs on Google Play services, which can send Google basic diagnostics, as our privacy policy explains.

Looking for a Microsoft Lens alternative?

If you used Microsoft Lens, which Microsoft has retired, AONE Scanner covers what most people used it for: point the camera at a page, get a straight, clean scan, and send it as a PDF or JPG. It saves on your phone instead of a cloud account, and adds a few things Lens didn't have:

  • Several papers in one shot, and open books split into pages.
  • Signing and marking up pages, with a signature you make once.
  • Photos and signatures at the exact pixels and KB that exam, job and visa forms ask for.
  • A choice of file size before you share, small enough for WhatsApp and email.

Free, for real

Many scanner apps ask you to sign in, show ads, or keep useful features behind a subscription. AONE Scanner is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases. It's made by amnLabs, a one-person studio in India.

Questions

What if I change phones?

Your documents live on your phone, so before you switch, share or download the PDFs you want to keep.

Does AONE Scanner work on older or low-end phones?

It runs on Android 7.0 and later.
