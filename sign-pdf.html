Sign a PDF on your Android phone, without uploading it

A guide to AONE Scanner's Markup

Someone sends you a PDF to sign and return, or hands you a form on paper. Most online signing tools want you to upload the document first. With AONE Scanner the document and your signature stay on your phone; only the signed file you choose to send leaves it.

Steps

  1. Open the document. In AONE Scanner, tap the import button and choose Import PDF or files, or scan the paper with the camera.
  2. Tap Sign. Open the document and tap the signature button at the top (or open a page and choose Sign).
  3. Make your signature once. Draw new lets you sign with your finger or a stylus, on a transparent, white or coloured background. Scan one photographs a signature you made on paper and keeps only the ink. Either way it's saved in Your signatures for next time.
  4. Place it. Tap your signature and it lands on the page, selected. Drag to move it, pull a corner to resize it, and use the top handle to turn it. Need it on page 2? Use the arrows at the top to go to that page.
  5. Add the date or your name. Choose the Text tool, then Today's date or My name, and drop it beside your signature.
  6. Send it. Tap Done, then Export or Share. In the PDF your ink, highlights and shapes stay sharp at any zoom.

More you can do on a page

  • Pen, highlighter and pencil in any colour and size; stylus pressure is supported.
  • Two erasers: one removes a whole line with a touch, the other erases exactly where you rub.
  • Arrows, lines, boxes and circles, or hold at the end of a line to straighten it.
  • Add a stamp, logo or photo from your gallery and place it like a signature.
  • Select with a lasso to move, resize, recolour, duplicate or delete.
  • Zoom in to write small; undo and redo as much as you like.

Questions

Is this a legally valid signature?

It's a picture of your signature on the page, like signing a printout and scanning it. Many offices and forms accept that. It isn't a certificate-based digital signature (such as Aadhaar eSign or a DSC), so check if your document needs one.

Can I still select the text of a PDF I imported?

Imported PDFs become page images, so their text can't be selected afterwards. What you add stays sharp, and the pages look the same.

Can I share my signature as an image?

Yes. In Your signatures, long-press a signature and choose Share: a transparent PNG, or a JPG on the background you chose.
