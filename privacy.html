Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026

AONE Scanner is a document scanner app for Android made by amnLabs ("amnLabs", "we", "us"). amnLabs is run by Ayush Naik, an independent developer based in India. This policy explains how personal data is handled when you use the AONE Scanner app ("the app"), contact us, or visit this website. It applies to users everywhere.

  • Your documents stay on your phone. Scanning and editing happen on the device, and documents are never uploaded.
  • No account, no ads, no advertising ID. We receive no data from the app.
  • Google's on-device machine-learning runtime, which the app uses to find document edges, sends Google limited technical usage data, never your images (section 3).
  • We never sell or share personal data.

1. Who is responsible

amnLabs, run by Ayush Naik in India, is responsible for the personal data described in this policy (the "controller" under data protection laws such as the GDPR). Contact: amnlabs.official@gmail.com.

2. The app

What the app doesn't do. The app does not send your documents, photos or any personal details to amnLabs or to anyone else, and amnLabs receives no data from it. It has no account system and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts or location. It contains no ads, does not use an advertising ID, and has no analytics or crash-reporting software of its own.

Camera. The app uses your camera only while the scanner is open, to find the edges of documents and take photos. The camera preview is processed in real time on your phone and is not recorded, saved or sent anywhere. Only the photos you choose to take are kept. The app does not detect or analyse faces and does not process biometric data.

Your documents. Scans, imported photos and PDFs may include sensitive documents such as identity cards, bills or certificates. They are processed on your phone (edge detection, cropping, enhancement) and stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot access. When you import a file, Android's file picker gives the app access only to the files you choose. Documents are not included in Android's cloud backup. We cannot see, access or recover your documents, and they are never used to train our models.

Sharing is your choice. A document leaves the app only when you share or save it, for example to a messaging app, email or your Downloads folder. The app or service you choose handles it under its own terms and privacy policy.

Permissions. The only permission the app asks you for is the camera.

Deleting your data. Delete documents in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your phone. We hold no copy anywhere.

3. Google's machine-learning runtime

To find the edges of documents, the app runs its page-detection model with LiteRT, a machine-learning runtime that Google provides through Google Play services. Your camera images and documents are processed entirely on your phone and are never sent to Google.

The runtime does send Google technical data about how it runs, which Google says it uses for diagnostics and usage analytics:

  • device information (manufacturer, model, operating system version and build) and the machine-learning hardware available on the device;
  • a device identifier;
  • app information (package name and app version);
  • the runtime's configuration, the type of event (such as loading the model or running it), error codes and performance measurements.

Google uses this data to measure performance, debug, maintain and improve the runtime, and to detect misuse or abuse, under the Google Privacy Policy. The runtime may also contact Google's servers to receive updates such as bug fixes and hardware compatibility information. amnLabs does not receive this data. Where the GDPR applies, we use this runtime on the basis of our legitimate interest in providing fast, reliable page detection on your device. The app has no setting to turn this off; if you don't want this data sent, please don't use the app.

4. When you contact us

If you email us, for example with "Send feedback" in the app, we receive your email address, your message, anything you attach, and the usual email details such as the date. We use them only to read and answer your message and to improve the app based on your feedback.

  • Legal basis (where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies): our legitimate interest in answering you, or steps you ask us to take.
  • Retention: we keep emails for as long as they are useful to support you and improve the app, for example to follow up on a problem you reported or to see the history of your earlier requests, and delete them once they are no longer needed for that. You can ask us to delete your emails at any time and we will, unless we must keep them to establish or defend a legal claim.
  • Where: our email is a Gmail account provided by Google, which stores messages on its servers, possibly in countries other than yours, under Google's own privacy policy.

Please don't email us copies of sensitive documents.

5. Google Play

You download and update the app through Google Play, which Google operates under the Google Privacy Policy. Google gives us aggregated statistics, such as the number of installs by country and device type. From users who have agreed to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, it also gives us technical crash and performance reports, such as the device model, Android version and the app's error log. These reports do not include your name, contact details or documents, and we use them only to fix problems and improve the app. If you post a public review on Google Play, we can see it and may reply there.

Update checks. When you open the app, it asks Google Play whether a newer version is available, using Google Play's in-app updates feature, so it can offer you the update. To answer, Google Play receives technical information such as the app's version and details of your device (for example its model and Android version), and handles it under the Google Privacy Policy. If you choose to update, Google Play downloads and installs the new version. amnLabs receives none of this information.

9. Children

10. Security

Your documents stay in the app's private storage, which Android keeps separate from other apps, and the app never transmits them. Use a screen lock to protect your phone. Emails you send us are protected by our email provider's security, and only we access them, to answer you.

Where the law requires a basis for such transfers, we rely on the safeguards provided by Google or, for your emails, on the transfer being necessary to answer the message you sent us.
