Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026

AONE Scanner is a document scanner app for Android made by amnLabs, an independent developer based in India ("amnLabs", "we", "us"). This policy explains how personal data is handled when you use the AONE Scanner app ("the app"), contact us, or visit this website. It applies to users everywhere.

  • The app collects no personal data. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Your documents stay on your phone. Scanning and editing happen on the device.
  • We never sell or share personal data.
  • The only personal data we ever receive is what you choose to send us, for example when you email us.

1. Who is responsible

amnLabs is responsible for the personal data described in this policy (the "controller" under data protection laws such as the GDPR). Contact: amnlabs.official@gmail.com.

2. The app

No data collection. The app does not collect any personal data, send it to amnLabs or anyone else, or store it on any server. It has no account system and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts or location. It does not use an advertising ID or any other device identifier, contains no ads, and includes no analytics, tracking, crash-reporting or other third-party data-collection software.

Camera. The app uses your camera only while the scanner is open, to find the edges of documents and take photos. The camera preview is processed in real time on your phone and is not recorded, saved or sent anywhere. Only the photos you choose to take are kept. The app does not detect or analyse faces and does not process biometric data.

Your documents. Scans, imported photos and PDFs may include sensitive documents such as identity cards, bills or certificates. They are processed on your phone (edge detection, cropping, enhancement) and stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot access. When you import a file, Android's file picker gives the app access only to the files you choose. Documents are not included in Android's cloud backup. We cannot see, access or recover your documents.

Sharing is your choice. A document leaves the app only when you share or save it, for example to a messaging app, email or your Downloads folder. The app or service you choose handles it under its own terms and privacy policy.

Permissions. The only permission the app asks you for is the camera.

Deleting your data. Delete documents in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your phone. We hold no copy anywhere.

3. When you contact us

If you email us, for example with "Send feedback" in the app, we receive your email address, your message, anything you attach, and the usual email details such as the date. We use them only to read and answer your message and to improve the app based on your feedback.

  • Legal basis (where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies): our legitimate interest in answering you, or steps you ask us to take.
  • Retention: we keep emails only as long as needed to deal with your message and delete them at the latest 12 months after the conversation ends, unless we must keep them longer to establish or defend a legal claim.
  • Where: our email is a Gmail account provided by Google, which stores messages on its servers, possibly in countries other than yours, under Google's own privacy policy.

Please don't email us copies of sensitive documents.

4. Google Play

You download and update the app through Google Play, which Google operates under the Google Privacy Policy. Google gives us aggregated statistics, such as the number of installs by country and device type. From users who have agreed to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, it also gives us technical crash and performance reports, such as the device model, Android version and the app's error log. These reports do not include your name, contact details or documents, and we use them only to fix problems and improve the app. If you post a public review on Google Play, we can see it and may reply there.

5. This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages and uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

6. No selling, sharing or profiling

We do not sell personal data, share it for targeted or cross-context behavioural advertising, use it for profiling, or make automated decisions about you. Because we don't track you, there is nothing for "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signals to switch off.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data: to access it, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its use, receive a copy of it, withdraw consent, and not be treated differently for using these rights. These include rights under the EU and UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act and other US state laws, Brazil's LGPD, Canada's PIPEDA, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and similar laws.

Because the app collects no personal data, the only personal data we may hold about you is email you sent us. To make a request, email amnlabs.official@gmail.com. We will reply within one month, or sooner where the law requires, and may ask you to confirm the request comes from you.

You can also complain to a data protection authority, such as the supervisory authority where you live in the EU, the UK Information Commissioner's Office, or the Data Protection Board of India.

8. Children

The app is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children under 13 or under the minimum age for consent in their country. If you believe a child has sent us personal data by email, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Your documents stay in the app's private storage, which Android keeps separate from other apps, and the app never transmits them. Use a screen lock to protect your phone. Emails you send us are protected by our email provider's security, and only we access them, to answer you.

10. International transfers

amnLabs is based in India. If you email us, your message is handled in India and stored by Google on servers that may be in other countries. Where the law requires a basis for such transfers, we rely on the safeguards provided by Google or on the transfer being necessary to answer the message you sent us.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version here with a new effective date. If a change affects how the app handles data, we will update this policy before releasing that version of the app and mention it in the app's release notes.

12. Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: amnlabs.official@gmail.com.
