Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026

AONE Scanner is a document scanner app for Android made by amnLabs, an independent developer in India. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

  • We collect no data. No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Your documents stay on your phone. Scanning and editing happen on the device.
  • Nothing leaves the phone unless you share it. You choose when and where.

Information we collect

None. AONE Scanner does not collect, store or send any personal information to amnLabs or to anyone else. It has no account system and asks for no name, email address, phone number, contacts or location. It does not use an advertising ID, contains no ads, and includes no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.

Camera

The app uses your camera only while the scanner is open, to find the edges of a document and take the photo. The camera preview is processed in real time on your phone and is not recorded, saved or sent anywhere. Only the photos you take are kept.

Your documents and photos

  • Scans, imported photos and PDFs are processed on your phone, including edge detection, cropping and enhancement.
  • They are saved in the app's private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read.
  • When you import a file, Android's file picker gives the app only the files you choose.
  • A document leaves the app only when you share or save it, for example to WhatsApp, email or your Downloads folder. What happens to it after that is governed by the app or service you choose.
  • Documents are not included in Android's automatic cloud backup, so they are never copied off the phone that way either.

Permissions

The only permission AONE Scanner asks you for is the camera, to scan documents.

Deleting your data

Everything the app stores is on your phone. Delete documents inside the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from the phone. There is no copy on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete.

Third parties

AONE Scanner shares nothing with third parties. Google Play handles downloading and updating the app under Google's privacy policy.

Children

AONE Scanner is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever starts handling data differently, this policy will be updated before that version of the app is released.
