Photo and signature size for online forms

Sizes checked against each body's official notice in November 2026

Exam, job and visa portals reject a photo or signature for the smallest reasons: 52 KB when the limit is 50, 201 pixels wide instead of 200, a PNG instead of a JPG. Below are the sizes 21 forms ask for, and how to make the exact files on your phone with AONE Scanner, without uploading your face or signature to a resizer website.

Make the files on your phone

  1. Install AONE Scanner (free, no account).
  2. Open the camera and choose Photo or Signature. Take the picture, or tap the gallery button to use one you already have.
  3. Under For, choose your form (for example IBPS or UPSC). The file is made at the exact pixels and KB, and each rule shows a tick.
  4. Not quite right? Adjust moves the frame around your face or signature. Markup writes your name and today's date on a photo when a form asks for it.
  5. Tap Download to save the file, or Share to send it, then upload it to the portal.

A signature gets a clean white background and dark ink, so a pen signature photographed on any paper comes out crisp. For portals that want it, there's also a transparent PNG signature.

Where a notice gives only a file size, the shape is up to you; AONE uses the printed size (such as 3.5 × 4.5 cm) to choose the pixels. Notices change every cycle, so always check the current one for your exam. A "live photo" is taken on the day or by the portal's own camera step, so it isn't uploaded.

Questions

Why does the portal reject my photo or signature?

Usually the file is outside the KB range, the pixels don't match, or it isn't a JPG. Some portals also check for a light background and a recent colour photo. Make the file at the form's exact size and KB range, and keep the background plain.

Do I need my name and date on the photo?

Only if the form's notice asks for it; many don't. When it does, open the photo in AONE Scanner, tap Markup and use Today's date and My name, then make the file again.

Is it safe to use photo resizer websites?

Resizer websites upload your face, signature and ID documents to someone else's server. AONE Scanner makes the files on your phone, so they don't leave it until you upload them to the portal.

My form isn't in the list. Can I still use it?

Yes. Choose My own size and enter the pixels and the KB range from the form's notice. AONE remembers it for next time.
